Privacy Policy.

What this site collects, why it is used, and where it goes.

Effective July 19, 2026 · Last updated July 19, 2026

Who controls the information

BNDR LLC, based in Phoenix, Arizona, USA, operates bndrllc.com and is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. “BNDR,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to BNDR LLC.

Information the site handles

Information you submit

The project estimate form may collect your name, business name, email address, optional phone number, project notes, business type, requested work, selected site size and add-ons, target timeline, budget range, the estimate shown, and the page from which the form was sent.

If you contact BNDR through a contact link or continue a conversation outside this website, we receive the information you choose to include in that communication.

Usage and device information

This site uses Google Analytics through the Google tag, or gtag.js. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies and receive information such as a browser or device identifier, browser and device characteristics, approximate location derived from network information, referring pages, pages viewed, interaction events, and visit timing. A successful estimate submission also sends a lead event and the displayed estimate value to Google Analytics. The site does not send the form’s name, email, phone number, business name, or notes to Google Analytics.

How information is used

  • To receive, review, price, and respond to project inquiries.
  • To provide requested services, products, licenses, support, and transaction records.
  • To understand site traffic, page performance, and whether the estimate flow works.
  • To maintain the site, prevent spam or misuse, troubleshoot failures, and protect BNDR and its visitors.
  • To comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

BNDR does not use project inquiries to enroll you in a marketing list or automated drip campaign.

Service providers and external destinations

Google Analytics

Google processes analytics information for measurement and reporting. Google Analytics uses a first-party _ga cookie by default to distinguish visitors and sessions. Google states that Analytics receives an IP address during collection but does not log or store it. You can limit cookies through your browser or use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Analytics opt-out tool.

FormSubmit

Unless BNDR configures another form endpoint, estimate submissions are sent by an encrypted web request to FormSubmit, which relays the submission to BNDR’s inbox. FormSubmit states that submissions can be retained for 30 days. See FormSubmit’s privacy terms.

Hosted checkout and platform links

Where a checkout button is available, it opens an allow-listed Stripe- or Gumroad-hosted payment page. Payment information is entered on that provider’s site and is handled under that provider’s privacy terms. BNDR does not collect or store complete card numbers or card security codes in this website’s code.

Other external links

The galleries, portfolio, social icons, source citations, and other links can open third-party websites. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies, not this one.

Browser storage and cookies

Google Analytics may set cookies used for measurement. The public site also uses session storage to remember that a visitor has already seen the home-page orb interaction hint during the current browser session.

The owner console uses local storage for an unpublished content draft and, if the owner enables temporary operator access, for the owner’s private signing key. It uses session storage for the current owner or operator login. These console records are stored in that browser, not in a BNDR user-account database. Public visitors do not have user accounts.

You can clear cookies, local storage, and session storage through your browser. Clearing owner-console storage can remove an unpublished draft or the private key used to create operator links.

Retention

BNDR keeps inquiry and business records only as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, maintain transaction and tax records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. Google, FormSubmit, payment providers, email providers, and other services apply their own retention settings and requirements. Browser storage remains until it expires, is replaced, or is cleared in that browser.

Security

BNDR uses reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to this static site, including HTTPS where provided by the host, a spam honeypot and timing check on the estimate form, session-scoped dashboard access, and signed, expiring operator links when that feature is configured. No transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; or to withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. BNDR may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

BNDR does not sell personal information and does not use this site to share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Browser controls and Google’s opt-out tool can limit analytics collection.

Children

This business site is not directed to children under 13, and BNDR does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through it. If information was submitted by a child, use the contact flow below to request review and deletion.

International use

BNDR operates from the United States. If you use the site from another country, information may be processed in the United States and in countries where the service providers named above operate, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Changes to this policy

BNDR may update this policy when the site, service providers, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will appear on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes apply prospectively unless the law requires otherwise.
